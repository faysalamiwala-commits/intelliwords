# IntelliWords 🚀
**Where Intelligence Meets Words.**

IntelliWords is an open-source publishing house toolkit for people who want to make books worth reading. From idea to next reader.

## What we do?
- Help writers write books in English
- Format books for Amazon KDP
- Design book covers
- Help Pakistani writers earn $2000 monthly from books

## Our Tools 🛠️

### 1. formatter.py
Formats your book title, author and chapters for Amazon KDP 6x9 inch.

### 2. cover_maker.py
Creates KDP book covers with title, author and color options.

### 3. price_calculator.py
Calculates your royalty - How many books to sell for $2000/month.

## Our Mission
Help 1000 Pakistani writers publish on Amazon KDP and earn in dollars.
